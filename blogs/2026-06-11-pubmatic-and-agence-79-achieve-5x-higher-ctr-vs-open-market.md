PubMatic and Agence 79 launched a programmatic video campaign in France for a leading mass-market retailer. The curated campaign leveraged PubMatic's direct-to-supply platform and premium video inventory and delivered a CTR 5x higher in comparison to the open market target, a 16.7% uplift in VCR and 12.5% higher viewability versus benchmarks. The results demonstrate how curated supply and greater transparency drive measurable performance gains.
