PubMatic and Amnet have launched the first agentic advertising campaign in France, powered by the Claude LLM and PubMatic’s Activate. Developed for INTERBEV, the AI-driven video campaign was activated in minutes using natural language inputs, reducing setup time by 80% and marking a major milestone in the evolution of programmatic advertising automation.
